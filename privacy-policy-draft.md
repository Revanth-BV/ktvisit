# KTVis.IT Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 25, 2026

KTVis.IT is a Chrome extension for recording browser walkthroughs for knowledge-transfer purposes.

## Information handled

KTVis.IT may handle information that appears in the browser content selected by the user for recording, including visual website content and the user's on-screen activity during the walkthrough. Session metadata such as recording time, chapters, and generated recording data may also be stored locally by the extension.

KTVis.IT does not record audio.

## How information is used

The information is used only to provide the extension's knowledge-transfer recording functionality, including recording, pausing, resuming, adding chapters, previewing, storing session information locally, and downloading the resulting video.

## Sharing and transmission

KTVis.IT does not operate a developer-operated backend and does not upload recordings or browsing content to developer-operated servers. The extension does not use advertising, personalized advertising, analytics services, or third-party data brokers.

The user may choose to save or otherwise share a downloaded recording using their own computer or other software. KTVis.IT does not control what a user does with a locally downloaded file.

## Local storage

KTVis.IT uses Chrome's local extension storage to retain session information and locally generated recording data so that the user can preview or download a completed session.

## Security

KTVis.IT is designed for local processing and does not transmit recording data to a developer-operated server. Users should avoid recording confidential information unless their organization's policies permit such recording. A locally downloaded video is an ordinary user-controlled file and is not DRM-protected by KTVis.IT.

## Children's privacy

KTVis.IT is not directed specifically at children and does not knowingly collect children's personal information.

## Changes to this policy

If KTVis.IT's data practices materially change, this policy will be updated and the Chrome Web Store listing will be updated as required.
